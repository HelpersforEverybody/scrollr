# Scrollr

**Browse any website. Watch its videos as a reel. Download what you like.**

Scrollr is an Android video browser that turns the videos on any website into a
smooth, swipeable reel feed — and lets you download them for offline viewing.

## What it does

- **Smart video browser** — open any site and Scrollr automatically detects the
  videos on the page.
- **Reel mode** — watch a site's videos as a full-screen, vertical swipe feed,
  similar to short-video apps. Swipe up for the next video; the feed keeps loading.
- **Downloader** — save videos for offline playback with a fast multi-part
  downloader (supports direct MP4, HLS and DASH streams).
- **Offline reel** — swipe through your downloaded videos as their own reel.
- **Built-in browser features** — tabs, bookmarks, history, search, incognito
  mode, and an ad-blocker for a cleaner, faster experience.

## Platform

- Android phones and tablets.
